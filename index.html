Research dossier

A course paper combining lived observation, interviews, and literature on ritual sound in Hubei.

Materials
Lived observation · Interviews with local practitioners · Related scholarship
Focus
The relationship among Daoist chant, daoqing-yugu, and local funerary music
Working insight
Local ritual music retains a Daoist procedural frame while becoming localized through dialect and folk melodic practice.
Limits
A small body of field material supports a provisional relationship map, not a comprehensive ethnography.
